Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

TAROON respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use TAROON and connect a TikTok account.

2. Information We Receive

When you authorize TAROON through TikTok, TAROON may receive the information permitted by the TikTok authorization scopes selected for the application.

3. TikTok Authorization

TAROON uses TikTok's official authorization system. You choose whether to authorize access to your TikTok account.

4. Use of Information

Information received through authorized TikTok services is used only to provide and operate the features of TAROON.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by TAROON. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

TAROON does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or as required by the authorized TikTok integration.

7. Revoking Access

You can revoke TAROON's access to your TikTok account through the appropriate TikTok account settings or authorization controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the TAROON administrator through the contact information provided with the service.
